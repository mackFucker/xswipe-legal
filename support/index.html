Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my XSwipe Pro subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose XSwipe. You can also open it from the App: Settings → XSwipe Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. XSwipe Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, open XSwipe and tap Settings → Restore Purchases. XSwipe Pro, themes and icons you've bought come back at no cost.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I get back a photo I deleted?

Yes. Photos and videos you delete in XSwipe go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days. You can also undo your last swipe right in XSwipe before you confirm the deletion.

Files you delete from a folder in the Files section are removed permanently once you confirm, so check them carefully.

Why can I only swipe 50 photos a day?

The free version includes 50 swipes a day in albums and folders; the count starts again the next day. Daily Swipe is always free and has no limit. XSwipe Pro removes the limit.

XSwipe doesn't show all my photos.

XSwipe needs access to your whole photo library. Open the iPhone Settings app → Apps → XSwipe → Photos (on iOS 17: Settings → XSwipe → Photos) and choose Full Access.
