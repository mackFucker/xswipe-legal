XSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the XSwipe app (“XSwipe”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. XSwipe is developed in Kazakhstan.

In short:

  • Your photos, videos and files never leave your device. We cannot see them.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase to improve the App.
  • We do not show ads, do not track you across other apps, and do not sell your data.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Your photos, videos and files

XSwipe asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos and videos, calculate how much space they take, and let you decide what to keep or delete. When you choose a folder in the Files section, XSwipe reads only the folder you picked.

  • All of this happens on your device. Photos, videos, files and their contents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Items you delete go through the iOS system confirmation and, for photos, are moved to the Recently Deleted album, where you can restore them.
  • Statistics about your library (for example, sizes and counts) are stored only on your device and are shared with the App’s widgets on the same device.

You can change photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → XSwipe.

Information we collect

Usage data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. It collects:

  • Which screens are opened, and App events such as starting a cleanup session, how many items were deleted and how much space was freed, changing the app icon, and the chosen theme.
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, country (derived from your IP address), and a random app instance identifier created by Firebase.

This data does not include your name, email address, photos, file names or file contents, and is not used to identify you. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the technical state of the App at the moment of the crash, device model, iOS version, app version and a random installation identifier. Crash reports help us find and fix bugs.

Emails you send us

If you contact us through “Contact Us”, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The App pre-fills your app version, iOS version and device model in the message so we can help you faster; you can remove them before sending.

Purchases

XSwipe offers auto-renewable subscriptions and one-time (lifetime) purchases. All of them are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. Apple tells the App only which purchases or subscriptions are active, so it can unlock paid features and restore them on your other devices.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers and do not collect a push token.

How we use information

  • To provide and keep the App working.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the App.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and do not sell or rent your information.

Service providers

We share the data described above only with the providers that process it on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC), for Analytics and Crashlytics. Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple, for App Store purchases. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Gmail), which hosts our support mailbox.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep data

  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, then it is deleted automatically by Firebase.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than 24 months.
  • Data stored on your device: until you delete the App.

International transfers

Firebase and Gmail may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

Your rights (including GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with similar laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • data portability;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We process analytics and crash data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and improving it, and support emails to answer your request. Because analytics data is not linked to your name or email, please include your request in an email to us and we will help you find and delete what relates to your device. Deleting the App stops all data collection.

California residents

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can ask us what information we have collected about you and ask us to delete it by emailing us.

Children

XSwipe is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit what we collect to the minimum the App needs.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App’s release notes.
